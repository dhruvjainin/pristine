# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, critique the work and not the person, and keep discussion on topic. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through [GitHub security advisories](https://github.com/maddhruv/pristine/security/advisories/new) or by direct message to [@maddhruv](https://github.com/maddhruv). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, or block participants who break these rules.
